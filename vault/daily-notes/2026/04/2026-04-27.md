#### Working with codex - again

[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/) has really fallen off as the golden child in the mind of developers at the moment. \
The "dumbing down" of opus 4.7 (at the time of writing) has been widely covered by influencers and regaled to me by friends and colleagues.\
For this project I've decided to use [openai's](https://openai.com/) [codex](https://openai.com/codex/).\
It's been performing adequately but I find it to be the most literal of the models I have worked with.\
It will not detect potential mistakes I have made (e.g. a typo in a filename or config option) as aggressively as [Claude](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview) has in the past. This is fine, I just spend more time checking my prompts before I send them.

If I had to summarise working with codex, it's that I spend more time context engineering.\
This isn't inherently a bad thing, but it is something I have noticed.
